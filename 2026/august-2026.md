# 3 August

I'm so nervous I could puke.

Hargrave Technologies has confirmed my technical interview on 6 August, 1 PM to 3 PM: 2 hours?! What am I gonna tell them? I don't wanna lie, I'm not the best there is. There are still tons out here who are more knowledgeable than I am. I just happen to know the Pytest framework they're using and have used it in my previous job quite extensively. But in no way, shape, or form am I the best there is! I bet there are tons in Australia who have learned this in their uni days, who have received proper training for this. While I, on the other hand, just winged it on the job. Studied the documentation as I was supposed to to perform the job I was meant to finish. I studied extensively through Stack Overflow, long way before any of these AI vibe coders started coding. I had and have severe impostor syndrome, and I know for sure that I will be the one to disqualify myself.

I'm a miserable case. I can go on and on and on. I can barely breathe at the moment, I might pass out.

Lord, I know I have prayed for this, and I am so grateful to have landed on this technical interview. Three years prior all these doors were closed; no matter how hard I yank each of them and market myself, I can't seem to open one. It's as if you, my Lord, have closed them shut as you did with the door on Noah's ark.


# 23 August

Lord Jesus, I have a confession: recently, I haven't been consistent in what You asked me to do. You asked me to read John, and I have this understanding that You want me to know You deeper, a deeper intimacy than what we had before. Ever since then, I studied the book of John and found profound truths that I hadn't realized before, truths that were right in front of my eyes, but were revealed to me in a whole new way. I am reading about different facets of Your personality I didn't know when I was reading the Bible as a whole unified library of books, and You became my role model (not that You weren't before, but You became relatable to me).

Yet, I have recently felt that I wasn't being consistent, and I am slowly and quietly being tugged away by my flesh.

I have been eating more (again). It has been a cycle that I've wanted to break for so long. I came to a point where I realized that I am not stewarding my body well, and it's not just a slew of body insecurities that I have to patch up one by one: it is spiritual. The Instagram influencer @darleneofthecrowd shared her testimony with Christ regarding her weight loss, but what caught my attention was that she didn't initially intend to lose weight (even though she clearly said she needed to). Instead, she wanted to pursue God more; thus, she went on a Daniel Fast and solely focused on You, Lord.

I also find myself spending more hours scrolling on my phone, Lord Jesus. My screen time significantly decreased from 9–11 hours per day to 4–6 hours per day through sheer dedication and reviving my analog hobbies. But 6 hours is still too much. At times I do not read the Bible at all, yet I have 6 hours to spare to consume content other than God's? I'm alarmed, Lord. This is not how I want to live.

Lord, I want to pursue You. Please make my heart long for You, to pant for You. Renew my mind, Lord Jesus; make me thirst and hunger for You and You alone. Cultivate my heart to have the yearning to seek You more. I could bluff and say that I have Darlene's heart posture when she first traversed her weight loss journey, but I don't want to lie to You, Lord Jesus. I could also say that I am doing well in stewarding the time You gave me, but I honestly need help. You, my Lord, can't begin to heal something that hasn't been fully and honestly surrendered to You. You long to see behind our masks; You want to journey with us back to the Father right where we are currently.

I need help, Lord, in stewarding my body, especially in the way I eat. For decades, I have let my appetite dictate what I eat, not fully understanding that man shall not live by bread alone. I have stuffed my body with processed food; I ate any craving I wanted under the guise of "I am a girl and I'm on my period; I have to let my body have what it wants" - a very worldly view, I now reckon. Due to my upbringing in scarcity, I never let myself lack anything, thinking that limiting myself meant I would be starving. Because of this, I battled severe body dysmorphia issues; I struggled to see myself in the mirror and often fantasized that this outward body was all a "mascot" and my real body was inside, waiting to be unzipped out of the suit. I have tried all the diets, intermittent fasting, and at-home cardio workouts, strength training five times a week, plans, everything - and they all failed.

It's terrible, Lord. I wonder why I didn't run to You in the first place.

Ah, I have a realization: this wasn't modeled to me by my parents, as they also suffered from this themselves. This also seems to be a taboo topic at church, and I have not heard anyone preach or talk about this out of fear of being labeled vain and idolatrous.

Due to my constant phone scrolling, I have abandoned all the creative hobbies that once filled my soul. I have neglected the write-ups You asked me to write, and this hurts me, as I don't often receive the vivid paragraphs and sentences You fill my mind with to write down. I am frustrated and disappointed, Lord.

In light of this, Lord, I would like to partake in the Daniel Fast: 21 days of diving into Your word, eating only vegetables and drinking water. This will be hard, but I am at the end of my rope. I want to be free. I want to seek You clearly. I want to long for You, Lord Jesus, and nothing else. I would like to prepare and start the fast on 13 September, after the birthday celebrations and festivities. Please show me what to prepare, what to expect, and what to do afterwards, Lord. I pray that You'll impart to me the grace to finish this, and the strength to turn from temptation and stand strong.

P.S. All I can see is the number 40 and the picture of Jesus in the wilderness. Help me to do this for 40 days, Lord.
